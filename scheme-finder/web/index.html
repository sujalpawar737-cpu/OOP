Find government schemes you may be eligible for

India has hundreds of central schemes across education, agriculture, health, employment and welfare. Many citizens never find out which ones they qualify for. Enter your details and this system shows the sample schemes you appear to qualify for, why, what they are worth, whether they can be combined, and where to apply officially.

Please note: results are described only as “Eligible based on the information provided” or “Potentially eligible; verify with the official authority”. The system never guarantees eligibility.

How it works

  1. Select a policy domain and enter your personal and socioeconomic details.
  2. The system checks the published criteria of every scheme and explains each pass, fail or unknown.
  3. It compares the estimated benefits of the schemes open to you.
  4. It checks the restrictions between schemes and recommends the best valid combination for your preference.
  5. Every scheme links to its official government source.
